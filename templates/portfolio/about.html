About Me

Management Information Systems & Marketing

Hi, my name is Samantha Bruner, and I am a soon-to-be graduate of Baylor University, where I studied Management Information Systems and Marketing. Throughout college, I built experience across insurance, marketing, finance, leadership, and technology, and those experiences shaped both my interests and the way I approach work.

During my internship with State Farm, I worked on social media marketing, customer leads, insurance quotes, and account analysis. I also served as Vice President of Finance for Kappa Alpha Theta, where I helped manage a chapter budget of more than $500,000 and supported the billing and financial needs of over 300 members.

This portfolio highlights the technical and AI-related projects I completed throughout my coursework, including projects using Django, AI workflows, machine learning, and other business technology tools. These projects helped me see how technology can solve problems, improve processes, and create value for organizations in practical ways.

In June, I will begin my career at Lockton as a Complex Claims Coordinator. I am excited to continue growing in the insurance industry while building on my strengths in communication, organization, problem-solving, and business technology.
